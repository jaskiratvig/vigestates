Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vig Estates LLC ("Vig Estates," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information about property owners and other people who contact us or receive communications from us, including by phone and text message.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Property information, such as the property address, condition, occupancy, and your plans for the property.
  • Records of your consent to receive text messages, including the date and time consent was given and your replies such as YES, STOP, or HELP.
  • Property and ownership information available in public records.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss your property.
  • To prepare and send cash offers to purchase your property.
  • To schedule calls, property visits, and closings.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive, and to honor opt-out requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and keep business records.

Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate, such as our text messaging and phone service providers, only as needed to deliver our messages and services. These providers may not use your information for their own marketing.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our SMS Terms for full details.

Other sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with a title company, attorney, or lender involved in a transaction you agree to, or when required by law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. We keep consent and opt-out records for as long as required to demonstrate compliance with messaging rules and applicable law.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your contact information, or to stop contacting you, by using the contact details below. Opt-out requests are honored promptly.
