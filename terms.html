SMS Terms

Program description

Vig Estates LLC sends text messages to property owners who have agreed to receive them. Messages include follow-ups on your property inquiry, scheduling, and marketing messages with cash offers to purchase your property.

How you opt in

You opt in verbally during a phone call with a Vig Estates LLC representative, who reads this disclosure before any text is sent:

"Would you like to receive text messages from Vig Estates LLC so we can send you updates about your property inquiry and offers to purchase your property? By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS messages, including marketing messages, from Vig Estates LLC. Message frequency may vary. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. We will not share your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes."

After you agree, we record your phone number and the date and time of your consent, then send this confirmation:

"Vig Estates LLC: You have agreed to receive SMS updates from Vig Estates LLC. Msg freq may vary. Std msg & data rates apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. To confirm you also want to receive marketing messages, please reply YES."

Marketing messages are sent only if you reply YES. If you do not reply YES, you receive only informational messages about your inquiry. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or offer.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent.
